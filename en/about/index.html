About Mainaa

Mainaa is the personal space of Himanshu Shekhar Pandey — a mechanical engineer turned product developer, web architect, and entrepreneur. With roots in engineering and quality assurance, and a journey that spans startups, freelancing, and product R&D, Mainaa reflects his passion for building things that last.

Background

Himanshu began his academic journey at the University of Manchester, where he studied foundation courses in engineering with a focus on electromechanical design. Returning to India, he pursued a B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering & Product Design at Apeejay Stya University, choosing advanced electives in composites, CNC machining, and programming.

In his final year, he worked on a Sheet Metal Fabrication project, applying AMADA CNC NT Series Punching and Bending Machines along with AMADA CAD for design and production work.

His professional career started at AIS Glass India Pvt. Ltd. as a Quality Assurance Engineer, where he implemented production standards, conducted PQCT and FEMA analysis, and trained teams across shifts.

Transition to Digital

Driven by curiosity and the urge to build beyond the factory floor, Himanshu transitioned into the digital world:

  • Entrepreneurship & Freelancing
    Designed UIs, developed web apps, and experimented with startups (including an online apparel brand).
  • Product Development & Management
    Worked with ADD24 and ClasstoCollege.com, building digital platforms, managing Google & Meta Ads, and streamlining content systems.
  • Mainaa.com
    Created this very site using Hugo, combining minimal design with functional product thinking.

Areas of Work

  • Web Development

  • Project Management
