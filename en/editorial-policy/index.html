Editorial Policy

Our standards for accuracy, sourcing, and corrections

At Mainaa, we are committed to maintaining the highest standards of accuracy, fairness, and transparency in all of our published content. This editorial policy outlines our guiding principles:

Accuracy & Integrity

We strive to ensure that every article is factually correct, clearly written, and free from misrepresentation. Information is verified before publication, and corrections are made promptly if errors are identified.

Sources & Attribution

We rely on credible, verifiable sources. Whenever possible, we provide direct citations, links, or references so readers can verify the information themselves. Quotes and data are attributed accurately and respectfully.

Independence & Conflicts of Interest

Editorial decisions are made independently, without influence from advertisers, sponsors, or external partners. Any potential conflicts of interest are disclosed to maintain transparency.

Fact-Checking

All major claims, data points, and statistics undergo a review process. Where uncertainty exists, it is clearly communicated to our readers.

Corrections

If inaccuracies are brought to our attention, we correct them as quickly as possible. Significant changes are noted transparently, so readers understand the context of the correction.

Our goal is to earn and maintain the trust of our readers by publishing content that is responsible, transparent, and accountable.
