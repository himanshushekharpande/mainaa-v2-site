Bihar's Biofuel Revolution: Ethanol Plants, Employment & Agricultural Growth

Comprehensive overview of Bihar's ethanol expansion under the 2025 Biofuel Policy

Himanshu Shekhar Pandey
July 7, 2025 August 25, 2025

Bihar Biofuel Policy 2025 Implementation

Industrial Land Allocation

Under the Bihar Biofuels Production Promotion (Amended) Policy, 2025:

  • BIADA will lease 25% of industrial land to OMCs and private suppliers
  • Lease duration: 30 years at ₹75,000/acre/year
  • Priority districts: Bhagalpur, Begusarai, Kaimur, Muzaffarpur, Barh, Jamui, Vaishali, Buxar

Employment Generation Plan

New Ethanol Plants Timeline

- 2025:
  - Plants: 2 (Bhagalpur, Kaimur)
  - Jobs: 15,000
- 2026:
  - Plants: 7 (remaining districts)
  - Jobs: 35,000
Total: 9 plants creating 50,000 jobs
